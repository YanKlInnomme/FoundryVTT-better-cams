# Module better-cams pour Foundry VTT

Better Cams conserve le rendu natif des webcams de Foundry VTT v14 tout en mettant automatiquement en valeur la personne qui parle. Les caméras au repos sont désaturées, puis retrouvent leurs couleurs lorsqu'une prise de parole est détectée.

Chaque joueur peut personnaliser localement la saturation au repos et pendant la prise de parole, la couleur du contour actif ainsi que la durée de la transition depuis **Configuration des paramètres → Better Cams**. Ces préférences sont propres à chaque navigateur et ne modifient pas l'affichage des autres participants.

# Module better-cams for Foundry VTT

Better Cams preserves Foundry VTT v14's native webcam appearance while automatically highlighting the person who is speaking. Idle cameras are desaturated and return to colour when speech is detected.

Each player can locally customize idle and active-speaker saturation, the active border colour, and the transition duration under **Configure Settings → Better Cams**. These preferences are stored per browser and do not alter how other participants see the camera dock.

If you like the module and have the means to do so, you can support me by offering me a coffee on Buy Me a Coffee (https://www.buymeacoffee.com/yank). Your support would be greatly appreciated and will aid ongoing development. This will enable me to prepare additional ready-to-play scenarios for you. In any case, I hope you have a rewarding and enjoyable experience. Please feel free to contact me with any comments or issues.

## Version 2.0.0
 - Migration to Foundry VTT 14.367
 - Full preservation of the native rendering of Foundry VTT v14 webcams
 - The module's effect is limited to desaturating cameras at rest and restoring colors when speaking
 - Added client settings for saturation, active outline color, and transition duration

Options are available in **Settings Configuration → Better Cams**. They are specific to each browser and therefore do not impose the same rendering on all players.
